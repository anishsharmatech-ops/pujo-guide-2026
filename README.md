# Kolkata Pujo Guide 2026

A single-page, phone-first guide to Durga Puja 2026 in Kolkata: pandal circuits with a journey timer, Metro Puja timings, traffic advisories, toilets and water points, and emergency numbers.

**Open it:** https://anishsharmatech-ops.github.io/pujo-guide-2026/

- Everything is in `index.html`, including the map library and a built-in outline map, so the text, circuits and map keep working once the page has loaded, even without data. Street tiles load when online.
- Facts carry source links (see the Sources section in the Info tab). Research as of 9 Oct 2026; items marked "Based on last year", "Unverified" or "Not found" say so.
- This is an independent visitor guide, not an official publication. Check Kolkata Police, Kolkata Traffic Police and Metro Railway Kolkata notices before you travel.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Walking times from the OSRM foot router on OpenStreetMap data.
